## 6. Asia / law — ICJ genocide ruling
- ICJ ruled 19 Nov (reported worldwide on 20 Nov) that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in the 2017 "clearance operations"; vote 9–6; first time in the court's 80-year history that a state has been found responsible for genocide (AFP, HRW, CNN, 19 Nov).
- Court ordered reparations and that Myanmar allow the "safe, voluntary and dignified return" of refugees; case brought by The Gambia in 2019; ICJ president Yuji Iwasawa cited ~10,000 killed and 730,000 driven to Bangladesh (Al Jazeera, AFP).
